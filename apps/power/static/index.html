On battery frames a second

Caps Forge's own drawing: the particles (in Forge and on the desktop), the visualiser and the games. Fewer frames means the graphics chip wakes less often, which saves battery. It can't cap other programs: GNOME has no whole-screen frame cap, so for the whole screen use a lower refresh rate in the Mode above. Settings > Speed and detail has these two sliders as well, next to the Browser's video and scrolling and the particles on battery.
