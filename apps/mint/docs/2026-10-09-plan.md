# Mint: plan (fake-money sandbox for money strategies)

date: 2026-10-09 · for: both · Relay task 126

## Why it exists

Emerson asked (WhatsApp, Relay 126) for research on how Claude could help grow
a starting investment (Plus500 CFDs mentioned, any path welcome), then widened
it: "build it out too, a simulator / paper-trading sandbox with fake money to
test strategies, plus anything else that could actually make money. He'll put
in a small real amount later to kick-start whatever wins in simulation. Keep
real money and any real account actions behind his explicit OK."

His numbers (board notes 1525/1526): about $70k salary, $3k HECS, rent+food
about $400/wk, take-home about $1,080/wk, **$300/wk** available once a winner
is picked. Risk comfort: not answered yet (assume "moderate": a 30% fall is
survivable, editable in Settings).

Mint never connects to a broker, never logs in, never moves money. It is the
place where a strategy has to earn the right to real money.

## The honesty rules (the point of the app)

1. Real costs on by default (spread, overnight funding, FX fee, brokerage,
   fund fees), each figure with its source in `venues.json`.
2. Every result sits next to the benchmark: the same deposits put into a broad
   index ETF and left alone.
3. No peeking: a strategy decides at the close of day t from data up to t and
   trades at the open of day t+1. A test runs every strategy on a cut-off
   series and checks the decision at t is identical.
4. Unseen data: results are split into "tuning years" (in-sample) and "test
   years" (out-of-sample); a parameter sweep picks its winner on tuning years
   only and then shows what that winner did on the test years.
5. Luck check: the strategy is compared with a few hundred coin-flip traders
   that trade as often, hold as long, use the same leverage and pay the same
   costs on the same prices. Beating 95% of them is "maybe skill"; less is
   "can't tell from luck".
6. Forward test: a paper bot's settings are frozen when it is made; it only
   trades on days after its creation date, so its record is truly unseen.
   Tried bots are archived, never deleted, so the number of attempts stays
   visible.
7. "Ready for real money?" is a checklist, not a button: beat the benchmark
   after costs in tuning, test and forward periods, luck check >= 95%, at least
   3 months and 20 trades forward, worst fall inside his comfort. Passing it
   only produces a plain-words plan for Emerson to carry out himself.

## Layout (apps/mint, port 8470, section "research", icon 🪙)

- `web_app.py`: FastAPI routes, `/shared` mount, static mount.
- `mint/engine.py`: daily-bar backtest core (cash, deposits, positions,
  leverage, margin close-out at 50% of initial margin, negative balance
  protection, costs, trades log, equity curve).
- `mint/venues.py` + `venues.json`: cost models, declarative. `cfd`
  (Plus500-style: spread % by class, overnight funding = benchmark rate +
  markup per calendar night on notional, FX conversion fee on P&L in other
  currencies, ASIC leverage caps by class), `etf` (ASX broker: brokerage
  flat/% with a minimum, no leverage, no shorting, idle cash interest),
  `savings` (HISA rate).
- `symbols.json`: instruments (Yahoo symbol, name, class, currency, CFD
  class), declarative. Starter set: VAS.AX, VGS.AX, IVV.AX, VDHG.AX, DHHF.AX,
  ^AXJO, ^GSPC, ^NDX, AUDUSD=X, GC=F, CL=F, BTC-USD, BHP.AX, CBA.AX, AAPL,
  NVDA.
- `mint/data.py`: Yahoo chart API (v8, no key; Stooq now needs JavaScript so
  it is out) with an on-disk CSV cache, refresh at most every 6 hours, adjusted
  close (dividends in) with open/high/low scaled by the same factor; CSV
  import for anything else; a seeded made-up series for tests and offline demo,
  always labelled as made up.
- `mint/strategies/*.py`: discovered, one file each, `META` (id, name,
  plain-words summary, long_only, params with min/max/step/default/help) and
  `targets(bars, params) -> list[float]` (wanted exposure in [-1, 1] at each
  close). Starters: buy_and_hold, sma_cross, trend_200 (above 200-day average
  or cash), momentum_12m, rsi_reversion, donchian_breakout, coin_flip.
- `mint/stats.py`: CAGR (time-weighted), money-weighted annual return (XIRR,
  because of weekly deposits), max drawdown, volatility, Sharpe vs cash,
  trades, costs paid by kind, time in market, worst month; luck check.
- `mint/paper.py`: paper accounts as JSON (atomic writes) in
  `~/.local/share/forge/mint/paper/`; manual orders filled at the last price
  plus half spread, stops and take-profits checked on daily high/low, funding
  per night, weekly deposits, a benchmark shadow, bots stepping on new bars.
- `mint/planner.py` + `paths.json`: "where should $300/week go": savings,
  index ETF (monthly block bootstrap of real history, minus fees), super
  salary sacrifice (tax difference, locked till 60), CFD trading with zero
  skill (coin-flip on random real windows with real costs). Output: 10th /
  50th / 90th percentile fan per path, chance to end below what was put in,
  worst fall on the way, rough after-tax figure.
- `static/`: index.html, app.js, app.css on the shared kit. Tabs: Plan, Lab,
  Paper, Ready?, Learn (key published figures with sources). Simple and
  advanced modes; sliders with number fields.
- `tests/`: engine, costs, no-peeking for every strategy, stats on toy cases,
  luck check seeded, Yahoo parser on a saved fixture (no network in tests),
  paper flow, planner, API smoke via TestClient.

## Known simplifications (to be written in README)

Daily bars only (no intraday); fills at the next open plus half spread;
stops filled at the stop level (gaps would make it worse); index CFDs on a
price index get no dividend adjustment; tax is an end-of-year estimate, not
advice; Yahoo data is unofficial and delayed.
