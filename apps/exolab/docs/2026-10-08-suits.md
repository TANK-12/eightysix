# Exo Lab: fitted suit shells (exo_full, exo_lite)

date: 2026-10-08 · for: both · from Relay task 104 (helper for Relay task 102)

## What was built

Two GLBs plus a sidecar JSON each, in `apps/exolab/static/assets/suits/`:

- `exo_full.glb` / `exo_full.json`: 23 mesh nodes, 54,878 triangles, 1.3 MiB.
  chest, back, abdomen, pelvis, helmet, plus L/R pauldron, upper_arm_shell,
  gauntlet, hand_guard, thigh_shell, shin_shell and boot. The model's cuffs
  (upper_arm_cuff, forearm_cuff) are thin bands.
- `exo_lite.glb` / `exo_lite.json`: 12 mesh nodes, 22,621 triangles, 580 KiB.
  back_plate, hip_belt, plus L/R thigh_cuff, shin_cuff, foot_stirrup,
  upper_arm_cuff and forearm_cuff, with skin showing between the parts.

Each part is one node, named `<part>` or `<part>_L` / `<part>_R`. Its glTF extras
are `{"part", "bone" (VRM humanoid name), "side"}`. Nodes have identity
transforms, and the vertices are in the body frame at rest pose: +X is the
character's left, +Y is up, +Z is forward, in metres. Materials use the four
slots `carbon` (matte dark, cfrp parts), `nylon` (light grey, pa12 parts),
`polycarbonate` and `accent` (emissive cyan lines). All materials are single-sided
(`doubleSided: false`); every part is a closed solid with outward normals,
checked by signed volume. There are no struts, motors or links; the page draws
the mechanism.

Sidecar keys: `model, character, frame, attach, glb, parts[name, part, bone,
side, area_m2 (outer surface), tris, fit_target (the surfaces it was fitted
to)], materials, material_slots, hide_rule,
notes, fit_check, build`. `parts[].tris` is counted from the written GLB.

## How it is built

The scripts are in `~/Models/exolab/`, headless Blender 5.2:

- `vrm_body.py` reads the VRM 0.x body (positions, triangles, skin weights)
  into numpy in the body frame.
- `suit_body.py` holds the surfaces and the hide rule. The envelope surfaces
  (`env_*`) are the visible faces minus faces buried under another surface.
  `shoes_outer` is the shoe faces that rays can escape from, which drops the
  footbed.
- `suit_scene.py` has the bpy helpers and the Workbench check renders.
- `suit_build.py` runs the pipeline for each part:
  1. Pick the source faces by joint weight and keep-planes.
  2. Subdivide once, then cut with planes for clean straight edges.
  3. Shrinkwrap to the envelope (or to the shoes for boots and stirrups) at
     `fit_gap_mm`.
  4. Push out any buried faces, then lift every vertex back to the gap.
  5. Split any triangle that crosses or sags toward the body.
  6. Solidify outward to `thickness_mm`.
  7. Add V-groove panel lines and accent stripes.
  8. Bevel the sharp rims; inner-surface creases are excluded.
  9. Run a final clearance pass that pushes any triangle nearer the body than
     1.2 mm straight out.
  10. Keep sided parts at least 1.5 mm off x = 0, so the two boots no longer
      meet at the toe box.

  `fit_gap_mm`, `thickness_mm` and cuff `width_mm` / `gap_mm` / `at` are read
  from `data/models/exo_*.json` and never hardcoded. Cuff `at` is the fraction
  along the segment from the proximal joint, as in `exolab/arm.py`. The script
  exports the GLBs, writes the sidecars, saves a new `.blend` to
  `~/Models/exolab/blends/` and renders checks to `~/Models/exolab/checks/`.
- `suit_check.py` (numpy only, no Blender) reads each GLB, prints the nodes,
  extras, triangle counts and bounding boxes, runs the fit and sanity checks,
  and writes `fit_check` into the sidecar.

To rebuild (about 20 s for the build and 30 s for the check):

```
cd ~/Models/exolab && blender -b --factory-startup --python suit_build.py
cd ~/Forge/apps/exolab && .venv/bin/python ~/Models/exolab/suit_check.py
```

Optional environment variables: `EXO_MODELS=exo_full,exo_lite`,
`EXO_PARTS=boot,...`, `EXO_NO_RENDER=1`, `EXO_DEBUG=1`. Always run the check
after a build: the build carries the previous `fit_check` forward until the
check rewrites it.

## Hide rule (dress)

The full rule is recorded in each sidecar's `hide_rule`. It applies to faces of
material `F00_002_01_Tops_01_CLOTH` on mesh `Body`. A face is hidden when:

- its dominant joint contains `Skirt` or `Sleeve`, or is an arm bone
  (upper arm, lower arm, hand) or `hips`, or
- its mean arm weight is at least 0.3.

exo_full also hides the whole bodice (dominant spine, chest, upperChest,
shoulders, bust), because the chest, back, abdomen and pauldrons cover it. The
skin under it was checked to be complete. The numeric threshold is also in
`hide_rule.arm_weight_min` (0.3), with `hide_rule.arm_bones`.

**The page must apply `hide_rule.extra_dominant`.** `static/game/character.js`
does. The critic found that `static/js/suit.js` `hideDress()` does not, and
without it the bodice crosses chest, abdomen and back (numpy test: 420, 357 and
130 triangles). That file is task 102's, so this was handed to them on the crew
board.

## Notes and assumptions

- The median inner gap equals `fit_gap_mm` by construction, because the
  shrinkwrap targets the same surface the check measures against. The
  meaningful numbers are the crossing count and the smallest gap. Some parts
  sit closer than their gap in small areas, for example the hip_belt 5th
  percentile is 3.8 mm against 6 mm, where the shell bridges a concave area.
- In the full suit, the upper-arm cuff (110 mm) and forearm cuff (130 mm)
  are the fitted arm shells, as the model file says ("the fitted upper-arm
  shell is the cuff"). So `upper_arm_shell` and `gauntlet` are only the
  stretches left between and around the cuffs: rings at the elbow and wrist.
  The shell's measured area (0.004 m2) plus the cuff's (0.028 m2) is close to
  the spec's 0.03 m2. If the page or the mass model wants one solid arm shell
  instead, drop the notches in `suit_build.py region()`.
- The lite thigh and shin cuffs are model shells without `width_mm` / `at`, so
  their bands (thigh at 0.62 with 50 mm, shin at 0.27 with 45 mm) are set in
  `suit_build.py region()`. The torso cut heights there are tuned to this
  character.
- The stirrups pass under the sole and reach y -0.0048, so they clip a
  ground plane at y 0 by about 5 mm.

- `foot_stirrup` (al_7075_t6) uses the `nylon` slot, because only four
  material slots are allowed.
- Hair (Hair001) is not a fit target, so hide it while the helmet is on.
- Hiding the skirt bares the legs. The page may tint the skin as a black
  undersuit.
- The stirrup gap needed a final clearance pass: long rim and bevel slivers sagged to
  0.51 mm under the shoe's inner wall at the arch (a face the open-shoe filter
  drops from the fit surface).

## Critic review (Opus agent, fresh context, 2026-10-08)

The critic confirmed the frame, extras, identity transforms, budgets, cuff `at`
and the fit_check freshness on its own. It found no crossings against the
full skin either. It reported nine findings:

1. `suit.js` ignores `extra_dominant`. Handed to task 102, see the hide rule.
2. The full suit's arm shells are rings. This is by design, explained above.
3. The boots overlapped at the midline. **Fixed** with the midline clamp.
4. The close spots on hip_belt, pelvis and helmet, and the circular median.
   Documented above.
5. The helmet crosses the hair. Already in the sidecar notes; `suit.js` hides
   the hair.
6. The stirrups go 4.8 mm below the floor. Documented above.
7. Sidecar `fit_target` was wrong for the boots and stirrups, and the arm
   weight was only a sentence. **Fixed**: `fit_target` is per part, and
   `arm_weight_min` / `arm_bones` were added.
8. The lite cuff bands are set in the script. Documented above.
9. The materials were double-sided. **Fixed**: they are single-sided now.
