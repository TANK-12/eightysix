## MEDIUM

### M1. The battery's power knob gives 20 times the energy at the same mass, so "power is not free" stops at the drives (CONFIRMED)
- Where: parts.py:352-356. `battery_wh` is multiplied by the part's power knob, but `bat_mass` is multiplied by weight only.
- How the UI reaches it: the Parts tab offers Power on the battery (editor.js:31). Its help text is the motor one, "A bigger motor: more torque, a higher top speed..." (editor.js:23).
- Repro: an inline profile with `knobs.parts["exo_full/battery"].power = 20`, sent to POST /api/model and to /api/run lift_cycle.
- What I saw:
  - battery_wh went from 360 to 7200.
  - total_kg stayed at 20.66.
  - Lifts per charge went from 2527 to 50543.
  - That works out to 3600 Wh/kg against 180 for li_ion_21700.
- Effect: session 17's promise ("power is not free"; the live README now says so too) holds for drives only.
- Fix idea: scale the battery mass with power, or call the knob "cell energy" and say plainly that it is free.

### M2. The power knob buys k times the torque and k times the speed for k times the mass, so a drive's power grows as k squared (CONFIRMED)
- Where: parts.py:183-196 and the `power` list in knobs.json.
- What the knob scales: torque, gearbox limit, top speed, rated power, and now mass and remote mass.
- What it leaves alone: rotor inertia (parts.py:195) and drive stiffness. A drive 20 times bigger therefore spins up as if it had the small rotor.
- J1 (harmonic_m) on POST /api/model:

  | power | mass | peak torque | top speed | rated power | reflected inertia, stiffness |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | x1 | 1.2 kg | 60 N·m | 8 rad/s | 180 W | 1.5e-5 x 50², 9000 N·m/rad |
  | x20 | 24 kg | 1200 N·m | 160 rad/s | 3600 W | same as x1 |

  At x20 that is about 192 kW of torque times speed at the output.
- Passive drives get the knob too, and gain only mass. The Joints tab offers Power on every actuator (editor.js:743).
  - free_bearing at power x20: exo_lite J3 goes from 0.15 to 3.0 kg and its "top speed" from 100 to 2000 rad/s, at 0 N·m.
  - The exo_lite suit goes from 10.97 to 16.67 kg.
- Does x20 on every actuator still help? exo_full in the tester:
  - Suit 194.3 kg.
  - Shoulder hold goes from warn to warn on all five poses. The wearer carries 5.4 N·m instead of 2.7, because the 33 kg arm sags the girdle spring.
  - The throw stays fail, now with 19 mm of misalignment and 1671 W.
- In the game, x20 still pays: sprint 5.84 to 9.94 m/s, jump 0.38 to 0.65 m, lift 12.5 to 125.9 kg.
- No crash, nothing negative, no nulls:
  - power x20 plus weight x20 on every actuator and every joint part gives a suit 160,000 times heavier (1,462 t). Every route still answers 200.
  - the x0.05 versions also answer 200.

  - The band does not depend on the pose. Moving the root 10 m and turning the hips 1.2 rad moved the same faces.
  - On the real VRM (VRM 0):

  - The twin shares the skeleton, the bind matrix and the position attribute, and it is disposed with the scene by deepDispose.
  - No automated test runs this JS; pytest only reads the sidecar values (test_core.py `test_suit_skin_is_declared`).

## Checked and found fine

- **N2.** Range sweeps are capped at 3000 cells. The worst allowed sweep (2701 cells) ran in 7.3 s. Lift and motions are capped at 1000 frames a motion; only six motions can run.
- **N3.** A 401-digit integer gives no 500 anywhere:
  - as a material knob, a part knob (+ and -), an override on a number, auto or text field, or as the name or notes;
  - on run, model, pose, derive, report and PUT;
  - 1e400 is caught too;
  - pose numbers get a 422;
  - a profile file holding a 5000-digit integer gives a 400.

- **N5.** Motions shorter than 0.1 s give a 400. Squeezed keys give no NaN, null or "no draw" text, but see M4.
- **N6.** Profile files holding a list, null, broken JSON, bad UTF-8 or knobs of the wrong type give 400s or warnings, and an inline twin of each runs. The exception is L1.
- **N7.** A gearbox limit of 0 (or 0.001) stops the drive, even with power x20: J1 = (0, 0) and the hold fails.
- **L-a.** Fixed text is refused: shape, kind, status, sex outside its choices.
- **L-a, KeyError mapping.** I swapped every actuator into every drive slot (J1-J3, elbow, hip, knee) on both suits: 624 calls across model, hold, lift, throw, derive, pose and range sweep. All gave 200 with no nulls.

- **L-b, L-d, L-f.**

  - L-d: `Object.hasOwn` is used in route().
  - L-f: a failed run hides the game stats and the part table, and the next good run shows them again (tester.js:224); a failed pose clears the reading.
