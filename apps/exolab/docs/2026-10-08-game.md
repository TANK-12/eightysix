# Exo Lab game: first pass

date: 2026-10-08 · for: both (Emerson and Claude) · Relay task 106 (a helper for task 102)

The game half of Exo Lab: a third-person exo soldier on a test range, with
Advanced Warfare style exo moves and Crysis style suit modes. Every move
number comes from the suit you build in the tester, through
`GET /api/derive/<model>?profile=<name>`.

Open it with `game.html?model=<id>&profile=<name>` (defaults `exo_full`,
`baseline`). The home menu's Game button (task 102) links there.

## Controls

- **WASD** move, **mouse** look (click the view to capture the mouse, or hold the right button)
- **Shift** sprint, **Alt** walk
- **Space** jump; **Space again in the air** boost jump
- **Q** or **double-tap a direction**: boost dash
- **Ctrl** (or C): slide while sprinting, ground slam in the air
- **Left click** (or F) punch; **E** lift a crate, then click to throw it, E again to put it down
- **1** Armour, **2** Strength, **3** Speed, **4** Cloak (press again to switch off)
- **R** repairs broken arms, **Backspace** goes back to the start, **Esc** opens the pause menu ("Back to Exo Lab" links `/`)

PS5 controller (Relay task 115): the bindings live in `static/game/controls.json`
and the pause menu lists them. Left stick move (tilt a little to walk), right
stick look, ✕ jump and boost jump, L3 sprint (stays on until you stop, or click it again mid-run), L1
boost dash, ○ slide or slam, R2 or R1 punch or throw, □ lift or put down, L2
held walks, d-pad ▲ Armour ▶ Strength ▼ Speed ◀ Cloak, △ repair, Create back
to the start, Options pause (in the menu the d-pad or stick moves, ✕ picks,
○ or Options goes back). The first press only wakes the pad; with two pads
the game reads the one in use. Pairing: `~/Forge/docs/library/exolab/2026-10-08-ps5-controller.md`.

## Files

- `static/game.html`: the page, the import map (`three` r186 and `@pixiv/three-vrm`
  3.5.5 from `/shared/vendor/`), HUD and pause menu markup.
- `static/game/main.js`: boots stats, level, view, soldier and HUD; reads input;
  runs the fixed-step sim (120 Hz) and the third-person camera.
- `static/game/sim.js`: the game rules as plain maths with no three.js: moves,
  modes, energy, crates, walls, targets, sentries, lap timer. `FEEL` at the top
  holds the few game-feel numbers that have no physics behind them.
- `static/game/physics.js`: kinematic capsule against boxes, gravity 9.81 m/s².
- `static/game/level.js`: the test range as data (boxes, crates, walls, targets,
  sentries, checkpoints) and the wall kinds.
- `static/game/view.js`: three.js scene: Poly Haven ground, props and sky, meshes
  for the level, effects.
- `static/game/character.js`: the VRM soldier, the fitted suit, the dress hiding,
  the retargeted animation clips and the procedural overlays.
- `static/game/hud.js`, `game.css`: HUD (energy, armour, mode, speed, lap timer,
  messages, derived stats in the top-right corner).
- `static/game/demo.js`: the scripted check (`?demo=1`).
- `static/game/controls.json`: the gamepad registry (layouts, bindings, menu,
  rumble); `static/game/pad.js` the mapping with no DOM, tested in node by
  `tests/test_pad.py`.
- `static/game/stub.json`: a copy of `/api/derive/exo_full` (baseline, taken
  2026-10-08 after the suit landed) for when the API is down. The HUD says
  which source it plays with.

No change to `web_app.py` was needed: `/shared` is already mounted, so
`/shared/assets/animations/...` and `/shared/assets/polyhaven/...` are served
from there, and the brief's optional `/animations` mount was not added.

## How the stats drive the game

- Walk `move.walk_mps` (Alt), sprint `move.sprint_mps` (Shift), Speed mode
  `move.speed_mode_mps`; plain running without Shift is 0.6 x sprint (a game
  rule, `FEEL.run_frac`).
- Jump take-off speed `sqrt(2 g jump_m)`; the boost jump adds
  `dash_mps - sprint_mps` upward, which lands the apex on `boost_jump_m`.
- Dash at `move.dash_mps` for `move.dash_s`; slam lands with `move.slam_j`,
  breaking walls within `move.slam_radius_m` whose break energy is lower.
- Lift up to `strength.lift_kg` (Strength mode: `strength_lift_kg`); trying a
  crate over `strength.break_kg` breaks the arms in any mode (taken literally
  from the brief; a red banner shows it, R repairs). Throw speed is
  `sqrt(2 E / (crate + arm mass))` capped at `throw_cap_mps`, E being
  `throw_j` or `strength_throw_j`. Punch energy is `punch_j` or `strength_punch_j`.
- Energy bar `energy.bar_j`; each mode drains `energy.drain_w[mode]` watts,
  sprinting drains `drain_w.sprint`, a boost costs `boost_j`; recharge at
  `recharge_w` after `recharge_delay_s` without a drain.
- Armour `armour.hp`; Armour mode multiplies damage by `armour_mode_damage_mult`.
  Cloak makes the soldier translucent and the two sentries lose track.

## The level

A walled test range on Poly Haven ground with a Poly Haven sky (HDR) and props.
The lap course: steps of 0.25 m and 0.34 m, a 1.2 m ledge that needs the boost
jump, a 5 m gap to dash across, a 1.0 m bar to slide under, then a gate with a
plaster panel to punch through; a timer runs from the start line through the
checkpoints to the finish. Off the course: crates of 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 and
400 kg, a row of pop-up targets, and three wall kinds to break: plaster (75 J),
breeze block (180 J) and reinforced concrete (2500 J). These wall energies are
game rules in `level.js`, not derived.

## Animation

The Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0) clips (idle, walk, jog,
sprint, jump start/loop/land, crouch, jab, cross) are retargeted in the page
onto the VRM normalised bones, three-vrm mixamo-example style. The exo moves
the library lacks are procedural overlays on top of the clips: carry (arms
forward), slam (curl forward, arms out), slide (crouch clip leaning back),
dash lean, broken arms hanging, and fists.

## The suit

When `static/assets/suits/<model>.glb` and its sidecar exist (task 104 made
`exo_full` and `exo_lite`), every node is attached to the VRM bone in its
glTF extras with `Object3D.attach` while the VRM is at rest. The dress faces
are hidden by the sidecar's `hide_rule`: skirt and sleeve joints, arm bones,
hips, the `extra_dominant` torso bones, and faces with a mean arm skin weight
of 0.3 or more. With a helmet the hair is hidden. The full suit tints the body
skin dark as the undersuit when the sidecar notes mention one.

## Checks

`game.html?demo=1&speed=<x>` runs a scripted input sequence through the same
input path as the keyboard and sets `window.__exoDemo = {ok, checks, ...}`,
also shown on screen. Between checks it moves the soldier and tops the energy
bar up. The checks are: walk speed, sprint speed, sprint drain, slide under the
bar, jump height, boost jump height, boost cost, dash speed, the slam's energy,
slam against concrete, all four mode drains, lifting the lightest crate, throw
speed, refusing a crate over `lift_kg`, arms breaking over `break_kg`, a punch
knocking a target down, and a punch against plaster.

### Not verified (reasoned only)

- Frame rate on Emerson's Intel Iris Xe in the Forge WebKit window. Headless
  SwiftShader here renders about one frame a second at 1280x720, which says
  nothing about a real GPU. The page caps the pixel ratio at 1.25 and uses one
  2048 shadow map.
- Playing by hand with a real keyboard and mouse (pointer lock, double-tap
  dash, Escape menu); the demo drives the same action queue the keys fill.
- The animation clips' look in motion; only still frames were looked at.

## Assumptions (decided without asking)

- Plain running speed is 0.6 x sprint; `FEEL` (accelerations, slide timings,
  reach, sentry damage and range, hp regen) and the wall break energies are
  game rules, not physics.
- Arms break on trying any crate over `break_kg`, in any mode.
- `tests/` belongs to task 102 and holds only `check_ui.py` (a Chrome UI
  check that pytest does not collect), so the game's check is the scripted
  demo, not pytest. No Python file was changed, so black and pyright had
  nothing to run on.
- Seen from behind, the zone signs read mirrored (they are double-sided
  planes). Left as is for a first pass.

## Results

Final code, 2026-10-08:

- Node demo (`/tmp/exo106/demo_node.mjs`, scratch): 20 of 20 pass.
- Headless Chrome demo, run `r5` (`game.html?demo=1&speed=40`, live API,
  `exo_full`, profile `baseline`): 20 of 20 pass, no JS errors, no notes,
  suit 23 of 23 parts attached with the helmet, 2042 dress faces hidden. The
  demo takes about 5.5 minutes at speed 40 under SwiftShader.
- Measured in that run: walk 1.50 m/s, sprint 5.84, sprint drain 353 J/s,
  jump 0.380 m, boost jump 1.520 m, boost cost 1500 J, dash 10.57 m/s, slam
  4393 J (derive says 4387; the demo's own 1/2 m v^2 is 4393), mode drains
  214 / 1667 / 706 / 360 W, throw 6.56 m/s against 6.58 predicted.

Bugs found and fixed in the last session:

- The soldier sat about 0.9 m too low. The animation library's root node is
  rotated (Blender Z-up to Y-up), so the hips track's local y is a forward
  offset (about 0.08), not the height (about 0.92). `loadClips` now takes
  each hips key through the parent's world matrix and scales the VRM hips
  rest height by the ratio. Checked: shoulders went from 0.46 m to 1.27 m
  above the feet, and `r4-idle-side` shows the feet on the ground.
- In the carry pose the crate floated about 15 cm past the hands. `hold_gap`
  is now 0 (the 0.32 m capsule is already wider than the chest) and the
  elbows bend 15 degrees, not 25. Checked: hands at 0.24 to 0.33 m in front,
  crate face at about 0.32 m (`r5-carry-side`, `r5-carry-back`).

Critic (one Opus agent, fresh context, session 3) found 11 issues. Fixed:
sprint lockout (sprint restarts at 25% of the bar), a held crate passing
through walls, the crate kept after Strength mode ends, slam energy now from
fall height, a 0.2 m minimum slam height, lap restart and wall reset rules,
the demo reading refuse and break events and the crate's measured throw
speed, skipped checks shown, an unbuilt model (HTTP 4xx) falling back to
`exo_full` with a note, missing derive fields filled from the stub with a
note, a crate underfoot not liftable, jump while sliding under a bar ignored,
a throw dropping the cloak, the HUD prompt using the sim's nearest crate, and
Ctrl+key blocked. Not fixed, written down: the dash gap can be cleared by a
boost jump alone, `drain_w.idle` is unused, and a sentry kill uses the old
player for the rest of that step.
