### 1. Reach dome (S): the range map wrapped round her shoulder in 3D
See: in the range test, a globe of dots at arm's length (0.43 m) round the shoulder. Green means the exo follows, amber warns, red fails. The amber cells up and behind the head are where the tilted axis 1 nears gimbal lock. In pose mode the arm points into a dot.
Build: `shoulder_rom.run()` adds a body-frame `dir` to each cell and `gh` and `reach_m` to the map. `View3D.showReach(map)` draws one `THREE.Points`, `tester.renderResult()` calls it, and a Reach box joins the layers. Details in A below.
Risk: low. At 10° steps the dots look sparse up close; a later pass can draw one small tile per cell.

### 3. Gimbal-lock cones (S): draw the lock zone itself

### C. Gimbal-lock cones
- Why it is exact: `gimbal.solve()` sets the lock measure to |det[a1 a2 a3]| with a2 = unit(a1 x a3). That equals the sine of the angle between axis 1 and a3, the line from the exo centre to the elbow. So a frame warns exactly when the upper arm lies within asin(0.15) = 8.63° of ±axis 1, and fails within asin(0.05) = 2.87°.
- Core, `exolab/benches/__init__.py`: `layout(rig, fr, rules=None)` takes `r = rules or DEFAULT_RULES` and, for every kind but cable, adds `out["lock"] = {"warn_deg": round(degrees(asin(min(1, r["sing_warn"]))), 2), "fail_deg": …, "length": round(rig.body.l_upper, 3)}`. `frame_json(..., speeds=None, rules=None)` passes the rules on. `motions.py`, `hold.py`, `lift.py` and `web_app._pose_frame()` pass the rules they already build.
- Page, `static/js/view3d.js`: add `GEO.cone = new THREE.ConeGeometry(1, 1, 32, 1, true)` and `this.layers.lock = true`. In `draw()`, when `main && this.layers.lock && L.lock`, draw four cones: for sign ±1, and for (deg, colour, opacity) of (warn_deg, COL.warn, 0.12) and (fail_deg, COL.fail, 0.25):
  - use a pooled mesh with `MeshBasicMaterial({color, transparent: true, opacity, depthWrite: false, side: DoubleSide})`;
  - set `dir` = sign x `L.axes[0]` and `quaternion.setFromUnitVectors(UP, -dir)`; the apex is at +Y, so it lands on the centre;
  - set `position = L.centre + dir * len / 2` and scale (len·tan(deg), len, len·tan(deg));
  - raise the fail cone's opacity to 0.5 when `frame.issues` holds a "lock" issue, and set `canvas.dataset.lock = count`.
  `static/index.html`: add a "Lock zones" box with `data-layer="lock"`.
- Test first. In `tests/test_benches.py`, `test_lock_cones_agree_with_the_lock_check(reg)`: take the light suit with `shoulder.mechanism = lateral_gimbal`, plane -90 to 140 and elevation 0 to 180 every 5°, elbow 30. In each frame, take the angle between `layout.axes[0]` and unit(`layout.elbow` - `layout.centre`), folded to under 90°. It must be below `fail_deg` exactly when `check()` reports a lock fail (skip cells within 0.3° of the edge). There must be at least one locked cell (plane 0, elevation 85), and the soft cable layout must have no `lock`. The scratch run of this test found all 1739 cells agreeing. In `tests/check_ui.py`: the full suit's range test shows `dataset.lock === "4"`; take a Back camera screenshot. Pose mode at plane -30, elevation 140 (the tilted axis 1; lock measure 0.02, outside a normal human range) must turn the lock tile red and the cone bright; take a screenshot and look at it.
