## Findings (appended as confirmed; ranked in the summary at the end)

### C4-M1. Keys exactly 0.05 s apart are refused, with a message that contradicts itself (CONFIRMED)
- Where: exolab/benches/__init__.py:358-363 (`if not b["t"] - a["t"] >= MIN_KEY_GAP_S`).
- Defect: the gap is compared in binary floating point, so most decimal pairs exactly 0.05 s
  apart compute as 0.04999999999999999 and fail the documented "at least 0.05 s" rule; the
  message then prints the gap with `.3g` as 0.05.
- Repro (scratch EXOLAB_DATA, TestClient): POST /api/run exo_full shoulder_motions only=throw,
  inline overrides `motions.throw keys.1.t = 0.9` (key 2 ships at 0.95), or keys.1/keys.2 =
  1.1/1.15, or 0.25/0.3.
- Actual: `400 a motion's keys must be at least 0.05 s apart (two are 0.05 s apart at 0.9 s)`;
  same for 1.1/1.15 and 0.25/0.3. 0.901 gives "two are 0.049 s apart" (correct). PUT of the same
  profile returns 200, so the saved profile then fails every motions and close_fit run.
- The tests only use gaps of 0, 1e-300 and 0.02 (tests/test_third_critic.py:143-147), never the
  boundary. A tolerance (e.g. `>= MIN_KEY_GAP_S - 1e-9`) or rounding fixes it.
