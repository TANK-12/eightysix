date: 2026-09-30 · for: both · part 1 by Chihiro (ejb-73), default account, in a visible session

Nene (Relay task 10) started B3 and left only data (`data/kit.json`, `data/weather.json`, the `small_map`

## Where things are (all in ~/Games/eightysix)
- `scenes/levels/small_map.tscn` + `scripts/world/small_map.gd`: the level ("Ashen valley"). Now the first
  startable map in `maps.json` order, so `Game.default_map()` picks it. Other lanes' bot scripts name `test_range`
  explicitly and are unaffected.
- `scripts/world/small_map_terrain.gd` (`SmallMapTerrain`): 1025x1025 grid at 1 m from the maps.json numbers:
  noise hills, rim, town flat, roads on a smoothed bed, river and lake cut below the water line, craters. Splat
  image r road, g shore, b scorch, a town. 16x16 chunks of 64 m, a 1 m mesh near (to 240 m) and a 4 m mesh with
  skirts far. One `HeightMapShape3D` for collision (layers world + grapple_surface).
- `scripts/world/kit/kit.gd` (`Kit`): builders for every `kit.json` entry (rock, shrub, grass, lathe, wall,
  building, bridge, mast, hedgehog, rubble). `Kit.build(id, seed, overrides)`, `Kit.break_apart(node, from,
  impulse)` for B5. Meta on each piece: `climb`, `grapple`, `anchors`, `pieces`.
- `scripts/world/small_map_scatter.gd`: grass, shrubs and pebbles as one MultiMesh per chunk each (60 / 170 / 90 m
  draw ranges, fade), boulders and debris as bodies. Settings > Graphics > Grass and shrub density works live.
- `scripts/world/small_map_sky.gd`: sky shader with cloud deck, sun, fog and volumetric fog from `weather.json`
  (4 weathers; `level.set_weather(id)`); Settings > Graphics > Clouds works live.
- Shaders in `assets/kit/shaders/` (ground, water, sky, grass, foliage). Sources: `assets/SOURCES.md`.
- `level.grapple_points` (outcrop tops, mast top, ruin corners, bridge); `level.look_from("<viewpoint>")` and
  `follow_mech()` for screenshots; `level.ground_height(x, z)`.
- Tests `tests/test_kit.gd` (5) and `tests/test_small_map.gd` (8). Bot scripts `tools/bot/small_map_check.json`
  (6 viewpoints, the mech walking, a frame measure) and `small_map_perf.json` (one feature off at a time).

- The water is one plane at the water line over the whole map (hidden under land); fine while all land is above it.
- Walls are flat-textured blocks; no damage decals, no interiors.

## Frame rate, honestly
Readings on this laptop swing by up to 30 % between identical runs (GPU pinned at 1200 MHz, 70 C, nothing else on
the GPU; the desktop's own animated layer is the likely cause). Medium at the walked view: 31.8 / 35.5 / 31.2 fps
before the detail rings, 44.8 once after (`images/115`). Probes: the ground shader costs about 1 ms, solid scatter
nothing measurable, shrubs about 2 ms, two cascades about 7 ms (single probe). **Not verified that Medium meets 45.**
Measure with the desktop idle: `tools/bot_run.sh small_map_check --preset=medium`, three runs.
