## What changed in part 1 (Aiko), and why

1. `Ballistics.drop_at` read the drop one tick past the range (13 m for the dart), which swamped drag's
   effect; it now interpolates to the exact distance.
2. Flung armour is a named, capped `Debris` body (16 at most, like casings and decals).
3. Limp: the drive's top speeds now scale by `speed_scale` on the mech (`standin_mech.gd`), set from the
   worst leg; the old counter-force fought the drive and lost.
4. Wire anchor: player input no longer wipes a script/AI `reel` every tick; the winch **stalls** at max
   tension instead of reeling on; the cable snaps only on an overload held for `snap_after_s` (0.15 s), not a
   one-tick damping spike; the fan **prefers a surface at least `min_rise_m` (6 m) above the socket** and only
   falls back to the ground ahead (the terrain is grapple-able, so before this the dart always bit the dirt).
5. Climb: once started it holds until the chassis is over the lip (feet leave the ground while hauling, which
   used to cancel it), lifts until the lowest foot clears the lip (`foot_clear_m`), then drives over; `max_s` 4.
6. New `scripts/ui/combat_hud.gd` (`CombatHud`), drawn by the range HUD: magazine pips, reserve, loaded and
   next ammo, reload bar; range marks for 500/1000/1500 m from the loaded round's drop; top-down damage
   diagram coloured good→warn→bad per part, lost parts as outlines, hull % and a limp warning; grapple bracket
   on the surface in reach, cable length/tension bar while attached. Theme colours and fonts throughout.
7. Test range: five loose steel target plates 70-145 m downrange and a 20 m wire-anchor gantry (only its beam
   is a grapple surface). New bot script `tools/bot/combat_check.json`.
8. New tuning: `grapple.min_rise_m`, `grapple.snap_after_s`, `climb.foot_clear_m`, `climb.max_s`; `climb.push_m_s` 3.
