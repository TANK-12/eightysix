# R3: first person with the whole mech still there
date: 2026-10-03 · for: Emerson / Claude · written by Relay 34 (hey, Sonnet 5.5 high), brief `../2026-10-03-R3-cockpit-mech-brief.md`

## Try this
1. Press the camera key (or Settings > Gameplay > Starting view = cockpit) and look down: the hull, flanks with their grab handles, the snout visor and dart pods, the sub-arms and the 57 mm barrel are all drawn now, lit and shadowed like in third person.
2. Fire the 57 mm and the 12.7 mm in first person: the 12.7 flame leaves the sub-arm tip you can see on the left of the snout.
3. Only the pod shell around your head (canopy, pod tub, the two side panels and the two lower side plates) is left out, and it throws no shadow while you are inside. Wingmen and the third-person camera are unchanged.

Files: `data/mechs.json` (`cockpit_hide` replaces `cockpit_show`), `scripts/combat/mech_combat.gd`, `scripts/camera/chase_camera.gd`, `tests/test_cockpit.gd`, bots `tools/bot/r3_cockpit_range.json`, `r3_cockpit_small.json`.

## What was wrong (measured)
- The cockpit camera sits at the export's `cockpit_camera` socket, body (0, 0.33, -0.22). The pod around that point is closed: `JG2_canopy` (box -0.36..0.36, -0.07..0.70, -1.19..0.64) and `JG2_pod_tub` both contain it, and `JG2_sidepanel_L/R` sit 0.30 m from it at eye height. With every part drawn the view is a dark tunnel (images/488).
- The 014 fix drew only the barrel and the two sub-arms, so the screen was a spike from the top and two dark blobs at the bottom corners (images/481, 482).
- A part moved off the camera's layers still casts its shadow, so just hiding the canopy would have laid its shadow over the hull next to the pilot.

## What changed
- `cockpit_hide` (a short blacklist per mech) names the pod shell; everything else is on the normal mech layer and is drawn from the cockpit. Sub-arms, flanks, visor, eye, dart pods, coxae, receiver, barrel: all drawn.
- `MechCombat.set_cockpit_view(inside)` turns shadow casting off on the hidden shell while the camera is inside (cockpit view or gunner's optics) and back on outside; the chase camera calls it when it moves in or out.
- The eye stays at the pilot's seat. I tried two other places: **the snout lens** (the export's `eye_camera`, y -0.16, z -2.25 just ahead of the visor, images/489): nothing needs hiding, but you see only the barrel and the two long blades, no hull; and **pod shell hidden but only canopy and tub** (images/491): the side panels, 0.2 m from the eye, still fill the screen. The seat plus the full pod-shell list (images/493, 495) shows the most of the mech. Kept.

## Reasoned only (not measured)
- "Nothing flickers": the nearest drawn surface is 0.44 m (barrel) and the camera near plane is the engine default 0.05 m, so no near-plane clipping is expected; I did not look at a moving sequence for shadow shimmer. The sun's shadow split distances were not changed.
- The sub-arms are the dark columns beside the snout; they read dark because the flanks shade them. Same material and light as third person, no extra ambient was added.
- test_squad `test_the_squad_registry_builds` fails here (expected squad_size 2, got 4). Not touched by this change (data/settings.json has no squad edit); most likely a saved settings value on this machine. Seen, not investigated.
