date: 2026-10-07 · for: both · Relay task 54, three sessions: Emi, Tsubaki, Izumi (hey account, Opus 5.5 high)

Request: features Emerson will enjoy, picked from PLAYTEST.md, NOTES.md and the QUICKSTART ideas, each with a test
and a screenshot; one must be a search box on the settings page. Emerson raised it from five to six features.

## The six

1. **Settings search** (`scripts/ui/settings_search.gd`, box in `scripts/ui/settings_screen.gd`). Typing in the box
   at the top of Settings (Ctrl+F or / jumps there) turns the card into every setting and key binding whose words
   hold all the typed words. The label counts most, then help, group, page, section, key and option labels.
   Matches sit under a "Section › Page" button that opens the page, and each one is a working row. Settings hidden
   behind an off toggle stay hidden, as they do on their pages.
   **Verified:** test_settings_search 5/5; bot images/607 01-02 ("fog": 3 matches, "jump": 9).
2. **Your music folder** (`scripts/audio/music.gd`, `data/settings.json` new item type `folder`). Settings > Audio >
   Music > Your music folder picks a folder of mp3, ogg or wav files. "Your music folder" is a new choice for Main
   menu, Exploring and In combat, and it stands in for Soundtrack when the soundtrack files are missing (the friends'
   share zips). The share zip readme says so (`tools/builds.py` MUSIC_NOTE).
   **Verified:** test_music_folder 4/4, audio tests 18/18, tools/tests test_builds 13/13; images/607 03.
3. **After-action card** (`scripts/mission/sortie_log.gd`, `scripts/ui/mission_end.gd`). The end card shows shots,
   hits and accuracy, Legion parts shot off, your kills and the longest one, damage taken, armour left and a grade
   from S to D, with personal bests per mission in `user://records.cfg`. A kill or part counts as yours by the
   unit's `last_shooter`, which `LegionMech.take_hit` sets before damage so the final kill of a mission counts.
   **Verified:** test_after_action 6/6; images/607 11 (grade S, "New best: grade score, accuracy").
4. **Handler radio** (`scripts/ui/radio.gd`, lines in `data/radio.json`). The Handler calls each wave (count,
   types, bearing), your kills and long kills, parts shot off and what that does, the Löwe ranging you, heavy hits,
   low armour, and the win or the loss. Lines type out at the top left using the subtitle settings. Switch:
   Settings > Gameplay > HUD > Handler radio. **Verified:** test_radio 4/4; images/607 04 ("Undertaker, Handler One.
   Para-RAID is up. 2 contacts ahead: two Ameise. Nearest bearing 039.").
5. **Final-kill cam** (`scripts/camera/kill_cam.gd`, started by `scripts/ui/mission_hud.gd`). When the last Legion
   goes down: quarter-speed time, a camera swinging 75 deg round the wreck for 3.4 real seconds behind letterbox bars,
   and a caption ("Final kill · Ameise · 451 m"). Then the end card. Enter, Escape, Cross or Circle skips it.
   Switch: Settings > Gameplay > Camera > Final kill cam. **Verified:** test_kill_cam 4/4; images/607 09-10.
6. **Resonance blade** (`scripts/combat/resonance_blade.gd`, tuning in `data/weapons.json` "hf_blade", ring in
   `scripts/ui/combat_hud.gd`). Hold **X** with a Legion unit at the tips of the two forward blades. The ring fills
   over 1.2 s (yellow), turns green ("tuned, release") and holds for 0.5 s before drifting back to half. Release to
   strike: damage x (1 + 2 x tune) at 250 + 600 x tune mm penetration, a lunge, sparks and a rumble. The key is
   rebindable; no pad button by default.
   **Verified:** test_resonance_blade 5/5 (a real Ameise is tuned to and cut); images/607 05-08 (yellow ring,
   green "tuned, release", strike sparks; the report shows 1 hit from 1 strike and one Ameise part cut off).

## Bugs found by the bot runs and fixed (this session)

- **Blade found nothing on the real map.** The 3 m reach query at the tips returned at most 32 shapes, and the
  map's ground trimesh answers once per triangle: 32 GroundBody hits and no Ameise (probe run images/606). The query
  now looks only at the mech and hitbox layers and leaves out the Juggernaut's own shapes. New test
  `test_ground_triangles_at_the_tips_do_not_hide_the_legion` failed before the fix and passes after.
- **The settings Back button was pushed off screen** by the first blade key label (49 characters, the longest by
  far, widened the bindings card). Now "Resonance blade (hold, then release)". test_ui 8/8.
- **Bests of nothing:** a win with no hits listed "New best: accuracy 0 %, longest kill 0 m". A zero is no
  longer a record (test updated).
- **Bot runs wrote personal bests:** run 603 left a 23-second "best" in the player's `records.cfg`. Bot runs now
  keep records in `user://bot_records.cfg` (`mission.gd`). The polluted file went to the trash.
- **Kill cam anchors warning:** the letterbox bars are sized by offsets now.
- Bot script fixes (`tools/bot/a7_features.json`): no wingmen (they shelled the Ameise at the blades before it
  could tune); the blade hook in `small_map.gd` also stops the unit's walk (`move_goal`), or it walks off the tips;
  the kill-cam shots are timed in game time, since the bot clock slows with the slow motion.
