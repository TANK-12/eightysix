## The JSON (glTF axes: x right, y up, -z front; metres; same idea as B1's)
The Legion are **rigid parts in a node tree, no skin**: turn the part nodes directly.
- `parts[]`: name (= glb node), parent (solid parent part), node_parent, class (hull/leg/weapon/sensor),
  volume_m3, mass_kg, com, collider, hull (≤64 points, model space at rest), merged (small meshes, like bolts and
  spikes, whose mass rides this part). Parts under 0.004 m³ get no collider of their own.
- `joints[]`: part, kind, parent, pivot, axis. Legs: `hip_yaw` (hip_*), `hip_pitch` (thigh_*), `knee` (shin_*).
  Others: Grauwolf head_pitch, blade_raise ×2, launcher_yaw, launcher_pitch; Löwe turret_yaw, gun_pitch.
  The same kind/axis ride in the glb as node extras (`joint`, `joint_axis`).
- `legs{tag}`: chain `[{joint, part}]` hip → thigh → shin, `sole_socket`.
- `sockets[]`: name, node, part, pos, forward; also `socket_<name>` nodes in the glb. Soles at each foot's
  lowest point; Ameise muzzle_R/L + optic; Grauwolf rocket_00..21, blade_tip_R/L, optic; Löwe muzzle, coax, optic.
- `weak_points{}`: the names `enemies.json` uses → part names (optic_sensor, leg_joints, rocket_pod,
  turret_ring, rear_deck).
- `lods`: full and lite glb + triangles; `locked_parts`; `notes`.

## Only reasoned (not measured)
- **Masses are totals from elsewhere, split by geometry:** Ameise 10 t (wiki canon), Grauwolf 10 t and Löwe
  50 t (lane A1's drafts in `enemies.json`, no canon). Per-part mass = total × (collider volume × class
  weight: hull 1.0, leg 1.3, weapon 1.6, sensor 0.6) / sum. Change `mass_t` in a unit's GAME table and re-export.
- Colliders are convex hulls per part, so concave parts (the Grauwolf body's X profile) are filled in.
- Glb materials carry base colours only; the procedural weathering does not export (baking is B6 or later).
- Draw calls: the full glb has 221-307 mesh nodes per unit (one per part and material), the lite 72-112.
  For packs of Grauwolf, A4 should use the lite copy far away, or merge meshes per part.
- The m12 belly hoses were not looked at up close (my underside crop missed them); the part test covers them.
- `export/hi/*.glb` (build.py's own copies) now hold the m12 field livery, the last build run.
