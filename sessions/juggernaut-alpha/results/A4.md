### Note 8: the Juggernaut's legs crossing (done, verified)
- **Measured first** (`tests/test_leg_gap.gd`: the closest approach between any two legs' bones, hip to knee to ankle in the

  `_knee_swivel` turns each knee about its hip-ankle line (up to `swivel_max_deg` 55, eased at `swivel_rate` 8 rad/s) to the
