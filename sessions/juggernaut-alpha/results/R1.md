# R1: a ground Emerson likes, on every map
date: 2026-10-03 · for: Emerson / Claude · Relay 32 (hey, Sonnet 5.5), commits 46231f7 and the one after it in ~/Games/eightysix

## Try this
- Start Ashen valley, the big map, the test range and either test bench and look down at the ground (walk off the road, then climb a ridge or use the photo mode): the dirt now has patches of dry crust and dark soil, scattered stones, turf that breaks up instead of one smooth green, and roads with ragged, dirt-crept edges.
- The test range and the rough bench used a plain noise shader (the "worms" pattern); they now use the same ground as the two maps. The flat bench is real stained concrete.
- Contact sheets (before left, after right, six views: walking height and 30 m up at three spots, plus the chase camera at spawn): `~/Forge/sessions/juggernaut-alpha/results/R1-images/R1-<map>-before-after.png`.

## What was wrong (from the before shots, images/483-487)
1. **Mush at walking distance.** The ground faded to its own mean colour with distance (the grain fix of lane B4, from before the textures were compressed with mipmaps), so everything past ~10 m was a smooth smear, roads worst.
2. **One scale of variation.** Patches were 10-60 m wide or 2.5 m texture tiles: nothing at the 0.5-8 m scale a 7 m machine walks across. The fractal noise only spans about 0.38-0.62, so the existing blend masks barely varied.
3. **Visible tiling and soft roads.** 2.5 m tiles of a very fine texture; road edges were a clean gradient from the splat image.
4. **Test range and rough bench were not on the ground shader at all** (`assets/shaders/terrain.gdshader`, a three-noise blob); the flat bench was noise on a StandardMaterial.

## What changed
- `assets/kit/shaders/ground.gdshader`: patch-based no-tile sampling (two fetches, one beyond 70 m) for dirt, stones, turf, road and their normal maps; metre-scale mottling from three stretched noise scales (`mid`); stones and turf breakup follow the new scale; the road edge is pushed in and out by noise and its edges are darker; turf slightly desaturated; far detail kept (detail 1.0 -> 0.7, was 0.75 -> 0.35, fade at 90 m, was 45) and normal relief raised (1.0 near, 0.5 far, was 0.7/0.25).
- Textures: dirt is now **Ground105** and stones **Ground112**, both 2K (ambientCG, CC0, in `assets/SOURCES.md`, `assets/kit/textures/dirt_g105`, `stones_g112`), scales halved (4 m tiles, same pixel density). Gravel, turf, rock and concrete are unchanged. The old 1K `dirt` and `stones` folders (Ground106, Ground108) are in the trash and still in git history; nothing else used them.
- `scripts/world/ground_look.gd`: maps without a splat image get a plain black one. `test_range.gd` and `test_bench.gd` (rough bench) build their terrain with `GroundLook.material` and a palette in `data/maps.json`; the flat bench and its steps, ramps and boulders use kit concrete and rock, triplanar.
- Per-map palettes are kept untouched for the two maps; the test range and rough bench got small palettes of their own.
- Bot hooks: `look_from`, `show_hud`, `follow_mech` on the test range and benches; `r1_*` viewpoints in `data/maps.json` for all five maps; bots `tools/bot/r1_ground_<small|big|range|bench_flat|bench_rough>.json` (+ `r1_q_*` quick ones).

## Frame rate (Iris Xe, 1920x1080 off screen, avg fps / GPU ms; before = the ground at HEAD fec6d04 in a worktree, run alternately with the after runs)
The first version of the shader was 15-40 % slower (small map Low 86 -> 59, big map Low walking 58 -> 37). Reading one texture instead of two beyond 70 m fixed it: Low and Medium are now equal or faster than before, High is 10-20 % slower on the small map. The GPU was shared with other Relay sessions' bot runs during these runs (R3 and R4), so treat differences under 15 % as noise; the big-map Low numbers being *better* than before is not explained and probably partly noise. Ultra not measured (it is vsync-capped at 15 on this laptop).

## Not verified / only reasoned
- Whether Emerson likes it: a judgement. The ground is still the same family of colours (the palettes were kept); if the colour itself is what he hates, change `palette.ground` in `data/maps.json` (dry_tint, grass_tint, grass_amount) rather than the shader.
- Dusk/night weathers and the Ultra preset were not looked at. Windows/macOS export of the new 2K VRAM textures not run.
- The test bench's readout panel is not a CanvasLayer child of the level, so `show_hud(false)` leaves it on in the bench shots.
- Not done: ground-cover decals (pebbles, litter, cracks) and slope-aware dirt; terrain-curvature darkening in dips would need a baked map.
