## Plan (part 1, written before building)
Terrain3D is still Emerson's open question 1 (default: not installed), so the big map uses the plan's fallback:
**chunked height maps with Jolt height-field shapes, streamed.**

Measured first (headless, this laptop): the small map's ground generator runs at ~1.3 µs per grid point (1025² in
1.33 s); FastNoiseLite 0.12 µs per call. A 4 km map at 1 m in one grid would be 16.8 M points: ~21 s and 64 MB. So:
- `size_m` 4096 (32 x 32 tiles of 128 m; the planned 4000 rounded to whole tiles).
- **Height is a pure function of (x, z)** (`BigTerrain.sample`): noise hills, ridges and rim mountains plus the
  features from maps.json (roads on a smoothed bed, rivers and lakes cut below the water line, town flats, craters).
  Any tile can be made at any step, anywhere, the same every time, so tile edges match exactly.
- **An 8 m overview grid** of the whole map at load (513² points): far ground mesh, far height queries, placement.
- **Near tiles at 1 m** (129² points, ~20-30 ms each) made on a worker thread around every anchor (the player and
  any mech near it); each gets its own `HeightMapShape3D` (collision) and mesh; dropped again beyond a radius.
  Mesh rings: 1 m tiles near, the 8 m far mesh beyond (as the small map's rings, with skirts).
- Towns from the B3 kit at data positions, then destruction: walls and buildings take hits through the
  `take_hit` route the projectiles already use (CombatHits.receiver), break in stages into `Kit` debris.
- Checks: tests for seams (neighbouring tiles share edge heights exactly), collider == drawn mesh, determinism,
  streaming bounded, a body dropped across many tile borders never falls through; bot runs with screenshots;
  fps per preset.
