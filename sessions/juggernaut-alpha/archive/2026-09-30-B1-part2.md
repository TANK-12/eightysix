## Only reasoned (not measured)
- **Masses:** collider-hull volume x an effective density per class (`gamedata.DENSITY`). Hulls overfill
  concave parts (receiver, barrel cone), so the gun classes use low figures. The total is an estimate:
  override in the game's `parts.json`.
- **Actuators:** static need at a two-leg stance (half the weight per planted leg) from the rest pose;
  knee rams from the rig's ram pivots (moment arm about 0.1 m, so the forces are huge); hip yaw = 0.15 x
  hip pitch and ankle = 0.05 x knee. Test bench: 96 V bus, 65 % efficiency, 25 mm/s rams, 0.15 rad/s
  gear motors, 15 kJ/K heat capacity, 80 K limit. Game mode: 10 x the static torque, 6 rad/s.
- Sockets `cockpit_camera` (0, 60, 470 U) and `logo_R/L` (+-86, 0, 450 U) are placed by reasoning.
- No LODs or baked normal maps in the file: Godot's importer generates LODs (default on).

## The JSON, for A1/A2 (glTF axes: x right, y up, -z front; metres)
- `parts[]`: name, bone, parent, class, volume_m3, mass_kg, com, collider, hull (at most 64 points, model
  space at the rest pose). To fit a collider on a bone in Godot: transform the points by the inverse of
  `Skeleton3D.get_bone_global_rest(bone)`, then a `ConvexPolygonShape3D` under a `BoneAttachment3D`.
- `joints[]`: bone, parent, head, tail, x_axis (the hinge axis on the leg bones), y_axis, locked.
- `sockets[]`: name, bone, pos, forward; also in the glb as `socket_*` nodes on their bones.
- `actuators[]`: joint, bone, static_torque_nm, game_motor, test_bench. `locked_bones`: gun_yaw,
  subarm.R, subarm.L (supplement: hard-mounted, no traverse).
