## Try this (play the current game with plain Play; no new numbered version yet, see "Combined version" below)

1. **Flip:** charge a jump (hold Space / cross), hold **crouch (C / circle)** and push the stick as you let go:
   forward = front flip, back = backflip, sideways = side flip, how far you push = how hard. In the air, hold
   crouch to tuck and spin faster, let go to open up before landing. Settings > Gameplay > Movement > "Flip lean".
2. **Wire anchor arc:** aim (mouse / right stick): dots show where the dart will fly; it now falls and slows, and
   the launcher lifts its aim so it still lands on the crosshair. Reeling (G / d-pad up) pulls much harder.
3. **Sprint any way:** hold Shift / L3 with A, D or S: about 52 km/h sideways, 48 km/h backwards.
4. **Heavy AP round:** T / d-pad right cycles ammo to "Heavy AP (overcharged)": half as much kick again
   (1.5 m/s), 1510 damage.

### Emerson's notes (done, verified)
- **Mid-air flip** (commit `23bdc82`). Real physics: crouch held as a charged jump is let go moves the push onto
  the feet on one side (up to the furthest grounded foot x `gameplay.flip_lean`, default 50%), so the same
  launch impulse passes off the centre of mass. In the air crouch tucks the legs: the body's inertia drops by
  what the legs take off (`leg_inertia` pitch/yaw/roll, roll new) and the spin rises with angular momentum
  kept. The air leg-swing no longer swings against a flip's pitch. `standin_mech.gd` `flip_offset`,
  `_tuck_body`, `tucked_inertia`. **Verified** `tests/test_flip.gd` 4/4: half-lean front flip leaves at
  -4.61 rad/s from a 1.00 m offset (r x J / I within 20%), backflip spins the other way, no crouch = no spin,
  tucking raises the spin with pitch angular momentum kept within 5%. At 100% lean the nose digs into the
  ground at take-off (real: it spins faster than it rises), which is why the default is 50%.
- **Wire anchor with gravity, better aiming, stronger tug** (commit `42fbf6e`). The dart (8 kg, 140 m/s) is a
  real projectile: gravity plus the line it pulls off the drum ((m + lambda s) v kept, lambda 0.22 kg/m). Fire
  control (`solve_launch`) lifts the launch so the arc lands on the crosshair; `fly()` is the same flight with
  rays; a dotted arc (MultiMesh, pale = will bite, red = can't) shows it, setting "Show the wire anchor's arc".
  Line now 20 mm Dyneema (330 kN break, 0.22 kg/m), winch 240 kN stall, 300 kW from the power pack, 14 m/s drum.
  **Verified** `test_grapple.gd` 9/9 (new `test_dart_falls_and_fire_control_lands_it_on_the_crosshair`: a level
  shot drops 2.7 m over 60 m of line, fire control lifts the aim 2.44 deg, preview lands 0.00 m from the
  crosshair, the real flight bites where the preview said). **Not verified:** the arc dots on screen (no
  screenshot yet; part 2's bot run must look at it). Not done: the realistic-mass table still carries the old
  steel cable mass (Dyneema is ~60 kg lighter per anchor), left because B4 was editing that pipeline.
- **Sprint sideways and backwards** (in `42fbf6e`). `tuning.json` juggernaut.drive `run_side_m_s` 14.5,
  `run_back_m_s` 13.5, walk side/back 7; toggle sprint now cancels only when the stick is released.
  **Verified** `test_juggernaut.gd:test_sprint_sideways_and_backwards`: 14.5 m/s (52 km/h) sideways and
  13.4 m/s (48 km/h) backwards, 0 straightened legs in 1,440 leg-frames each, worst tilt 1.9 deg.
- **More recoil and damage: Heavy AP (overcharged)** (in `42fbf6e`). Honest limit: more kick needs more momentum
  out of the muzzle. A 4 kg tungsten slug on a 2.4 kg overcharge at 1100 m/s: 8,360 N s (APFSDS 5,580), 2.4 MJ so
  damage 1510 (same per joule), penetration 140 mm. **Verified** `test_combat.gd:test_heavy_round_kicks_harder_and_hits_harder`
  (8,360 N s, 1.50 m/s kick in the air). Barrel wear from overcharging is not modelled.

### The Legion's body (done, verified; commit `e2d5798`)
- B2's exports synced (`tools/sync_models.py legion`, 9 files, import 0 errors) into `assets/models/legion/`.
- `scripts/legion/legion_mech.gd` (`LegionMech`): one RigidBody3D per unit from `<id>.json` + glb. Mass, centre of
  mass and inertia summed from the parts (Ameise 10 t, Grauwolf 10 t, Löwe 50 t; the typed `mass_kg` drafts are
  gone from `enemies.json`). Parts with no joint above them are the body's convex colliders; parts riding a joint
  are hitboxes (Area3D, layer 3) moved with the pose every tick. Leg IK: hip yaw chosen so the sole's fixed
  offset along the pitch axis is kept, then two-bone hip pitch + knee in the leg plane; node transforms set from
  model-space deltas (`_apply_joint`). Gait: one clock, two alternating groups (tripods for six legs, 4+4 for the
  Löwe), cadence from speed / stride, feet land where the hip will be half a stance on (ray to the ground), lift
  arcs. Support: spring + damper from each planted foot to its hip (bounce_hz, damping_ratio). Drive and turn:
  forces through the feet capped by grip x the load they carry. Turret / launcher yaw and gun / launcher pitch
  follow `aim_point` at `gait.aim_deg_s`. `fire()` spawns a `Projectile` from a socket with spread and real
  recoil; weapons with a `wake` pass it on to the round (no wake physics yet).
- Damage zones (`DamageModel.add_part`, `core` kind = critical): hull, a leg per leg, knees (the leg_joints weak
  point, each also cripples its leg), arms, and weak points with `to_hull` pass-through. Löwe hull 240 mm (past
  the 57 mm APFSDS's 180) so it has to be flanked for the turret ring (40) and rear deck (25).
  `enemies.json` per unit: `gait` and `damage` blocks, status ready.
- **Verified** `tests/test_legion.gd` 5/5: all three build with mass = the export total; stand upright (tilt
  0.3-0.6 deg) carrying their weight with every sole on its foot (IK error 0.000 m standing); walk at the asked
  speed (Ameise / Grauwolf 8.0 m/s, Löwe 7.2) by stepping (81-117 steps in 5 s), tilt under 3 deg, IK under
  0.07 m while walking, quarter turn on the spot to 0.0 deg; an optic hit reaches the hull, four hull hits
  destroy and the legs let go; a 57 mm round fired at a Grauwolf leg hits its moving hitbox.
- Full suite at the end of part 1: run in part 2 first (last full run: 162 passed, 7 failed: 5 `test_audio`
  from B4 part 2's uncommitted audio work, `test_scripts` compile of legion_mech (fixed since, class cache), and
  `test_grapple:test_climbs_a_ledge_in_reach` once (passes alone 3 times: watch it)).

## Try this (version 008 or later)

In the field press **P** (or pause, then **Photo mode**). Time freezes and the HUD hides. Drag with the mouse
or use the right stick to orbit your Juggernaut, wheel or R2 / L2 to zoom, WASD or the left stick to move the
point it circles. **F12** (or cross) saves a PNG to `~/Pictures/Eighty Six/`. **Esc**, circle or P goes back.
