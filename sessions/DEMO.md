# Forge demo for Dad (about 2 minutes)

Everything new appears after Forge restarts once: close it with
Ctrl+Shift+Q and open it again, or tell Claude "restart forge".
Checked off screen with tests and screenshots; not yet tried in the real
window, so give each step a quick try before showing it.

## 1. Home screen
- The clock, the greeting, the tiles and the dock. The bar at the top has
  the ♫ music button, Aa (sizes), the clock and ⚙.
- Type anywhere on the home screen to search apps.

## 2. Hide and archive (right-click any tile)
- **Hide from home**: the tile leaves home and the dock, and a notice with
  **Undo** appears. Hidden apps sit in a folded "Hidden" section at the
  bottom of home, and search still finds them.
- **Archive**: the app is gone everywhere and never starts. Undo is in the
  same notice; later, Settings brings it back.

## 3. Now playing (the ♫ button, top bar)
- Play a video or song in the **Media** app, go back home, click ♫.
- The big card shows what is playing and which app: pause, seek, volume
  (up to 200% for Forge's own media), and **Go to it ↗**, which jumps to the
  Media app. Players on the desktop (Firefox, Spotify) show as rows under
  it; click a name to make it the big one.
- Below that, **Background music** (if it is on in Settings): its volume goes
  past 100% for quiet tracks, loud and quiet songs are evened out, and it
  steps aside by itself while another app plays a video, then comes back.

## 4. Full screen
- F11, or an app's Full screen button: the app fills the window with no
  bars; Esc or F11 brings them back. Works in all five looks.

## 5. New apps
- **Files** (a file manager with thumbnails, preview, drag and drop; delete
  goes to the trash), **Terminal** (tabs, htop and vim work), **Code** (real
  VS Code in a page).

## 6. The Claude app's new look
- In the Claude app, click **enjoy ↗** (next to "+ folder"). Projects,
  each task with its plan and a progress bar beside the chat, "running /
  needs you", notes and workflows.
- **roadmap ∞**: every Claude work session on Forge as a constellation;
  each thing asked for is a star that lights up when done. Click a star to
  see the words asked and what was done.
- "classic layout ↗" at the bottom left goes back.
