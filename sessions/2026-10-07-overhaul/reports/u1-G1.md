## Browser

Emerson: "the forge app browser lowkey never works for me".

**Root cause, fixed in forge.py (commit 97bfd48, `Forge views: ...`).** The hub
sends `owner` (which app is in front) and the bar `inset` before the Browser's
page asks for its first native view. forge.py dropped both while no ViewHost
existed, so the first page stayed hidden in every Forge session until another
app had been opened. `view_handler()` now keeps them and hands them to the host
when it is made. Test in `tests/test_views.py`. Reaches Emerson on his next
Forge restart (his Forge runs since 2026-10-07 20:52; never restarted by us).
