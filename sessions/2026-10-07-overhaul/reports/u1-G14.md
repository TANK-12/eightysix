## Assumptions
- An over-long id is "not found" (404), not "bad request": no such item can
  exist on disk. `/api/series/<long>/chapters` answers 200 with no chapters,
  as it does for any unknown series.
- watchJob gives up after three straight 404s (tries at about 0, 1.6 and
  4.8 s). Jobs live only in memory, so after a backend restart an old job
  is gone for good and ending with an error is the right answer.
- PanelForge's finite-number check is one app-wide FastAPI dependency: it
  re-reads JSON bodies (already cached by FastAPI), ignores bodies that are
  not JSON, and leaves malformed JSON to FastAPI's own 422.
