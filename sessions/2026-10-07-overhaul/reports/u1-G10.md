## shared/ recommendations
- `shared/forge.css` has no scrollbar styling; every app adds the same
  `scrollbar-width` + `::-webkit-scrollbar` block. One shared rule would do.
- `shared/forge.css` could carry `[hidden] { display: none !important; }`:
  any app rule that sets `display` on a block silently beats `hidden` (the
  Jan Tune Advanced bug).
