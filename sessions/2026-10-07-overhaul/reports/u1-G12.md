## Dossier (commit 4416aa7)

What changed:
- Backend path safety: `store.subject_dir` raises `store.BadSlug` for an empty,
  `.`/`..` or slash-holding slug (answered as 404). `DELETE /api/subject/%2E%2E`
  used to resolve to `subjects/..`, the whole library. `dossier.delete` also only
  removes a slug that `slugify` could have produced (all 2,450 real folders pass).
- Wrong-typed numbers are a 400 instead of a 500/connection reset: queue
  recipe/deepen `limit`, briefing `days` (clamped 1-3650), watch
  `every_hours`/`per_sweep`, history `limit`. Unknown briefing kind: 400/404.
  (These edits were left untested by the 3rd stopped worker; reviewed, kept,
  black-formatted and tested.)
- Rest when hidden + break 6: queue watcher (4 s) and history watcher (3 s) now
  run through `ForgeIdle.poll` with `{ backoff: 2, maxMs: 30000 }`; each job
  returns false on failure; the jobs card says the server is not answering.
- Looks: top bar wraps (no sideways scroll at 760 px), thin themed scrollbars.

## Foresight (commit 6f78d95)

  of 2,450. `dossier_link` now follows Dossier's own Location choice

  as objects with a valid slug. `store.check_slug` guards `series_path`,
  `sources_for` and `/api/subject/{slug}`; watchlist/findings limits clamped.
- Rest when hidden + break 6: the sweep poll (8 s idle, 1.5 s while sweeping)
  runs through `ForgeIdle.poll` with backoff; before, it ran while hidden and a
  single failed read stopped it for good.
- Looks: top bar wraps (no sideways scroll at 760/1100 px), thin scrollbars.

## Critic

One opus critic, fresh context, own rig on 9025/9026. Full report:
`sessions/2026-10-07-overhaul/critics/u1-G12-critic.md`. All six findings
verified by it; five were code and are fixed in the follow-up commits, the
sixth was my evidence (corrected above).

1. Dossier "Get new info" polled `/api/history` every 1.5 s while hidden and
   gave up with a raw alert if the server blinked. **Fixed:** it runs through
   the same `watch()`/`ForgeIdle.poll` as the history card; a failed read backs
   off instead of alerting. Verified with the critic's repro (fetch faked):
   hidden 10 s, 0 timers and 0 fetches (before 6); a second run with one failed
   read in the middle still finished and showed "Added 7 news items and a fresh
   copy of the article", no dialog.
2. Foresight: a failed `/api/status` at load left the page dead and Sweep threw
   "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'now')". **Fixed:** the sweep poll
   is made first thing in `boot()`, and a failed boot retries itself on a
   ForgeIdle poll (4 s, stretching to 30 s, resting while hidden). Verified
   with the critic's `fake-statusdown.js`: pill "backend unreachable" at load,
   then Sweep clicked: no dialog, status re-read and the pills filled in
   ("Jan offline · rules tier still works", storage pill), sweep progress read.
3. Foresight tests followed Emerson's real Dossier choice and left a write
   probe in `~/AN3-backup/ForesightData` on every run. **Fixed:** an autouse
   fixture in `tests/conftest.py` points the Dossier choice, USB and local
   roots at a temp folder for every test, plus a test that the data root is
   inside it. Verified: that folder's mtime was 01:35:20 before and after a
   full `pytest` run.
4. Foresight took slugs of any length (200 kB slug started a sweep and bloated
   its log; `POST /api/plans` was a 500) and a long unbroken log line pushed
   the Signals view sideways. **Fixed:** `MAX_SLUG = 200` in both apps'
   `store.py` (longest real slug is 128): `check_slug`/`subject_dir` refuse
   longer, `slugify` cuts to it, `/api/plans` and `/api/plans/learn` check the
   slug; CSS `minmax(0,1fr)` in the narrow layout and `overflow-wrap: anywhere`
   on `.joblog`. Verified: on scratch servers the 200 kB plan is a 404 and the
   200 kB sweep a 400; a 200 kB log line plus a 3 kB URL in `#sweep-log` gives
   no page overflow at 760/1100/1500; tests added in both apps.
5. Triple clicks sent three POSTs (Dossier deepen queued 75 deep dives instead
   of 25). **Fixed:** Dossier queue buttons (start, pause, stop, clear,
   deepen) and history start, and Foresight "score plans", stay disabled while
   their request is out. Verified with the critic's `dbl.js` + `fake-post.js`:
   no button on either page sends more than one POST now (before: q-deepen,
   q-start, q-pause, history-start, plan-score each 3).
6. My screenshots per view were the same picture. Corrected above.
