## Shared/ recommendations
- `shared/forge_chrome.js` `_injectClose`: when the app's own
  `#go-fullscreen` is `position: fixed` (Cosmos), the inline Close app
  button lands in the page flow at the top left of the body, over whatever
  is there. Cosmos now pins it in its own CSS; a shared fix would place the
  chip fixed beside a fixed Full screen button. Other apps with a fixed
  `#go-fullscreen` likely have the same overlap.
- The check scripts import `tests/cdp_driver.py` through `sys.path`, so
  pyright cannot see it; each check_ui now carries a
  `pyright: ignore[reportMissingImports]`. A per-app `pyrightconfig.json`
  with `extraPaths: ["../../tests"]` would be cleaner hub-wide.
- Harness note for other check scripts: a fetch blocker written with
  `Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` must read `location.search` per
  call; an iframe's first blank document shares the window, so a value read
  once comes from `about:blank`.

## Assumptions
- 30 fps for the menu sky is enough (slow twinkle); puzzles keep their own
  frame-rate setting (power saver 24 / smooth).
- A 5 s window for refusing a repeated launch is long enough for a double
  click and short enough not to block a real second launch.
- Eighty Six's one-shot `setTimeout(loadVersions, 15000)` after Play was
  left as is: one fetch, even if hidden.
