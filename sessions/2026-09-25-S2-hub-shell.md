  shows a close control in a consistent place (for example top-right of the

  Alt+Left/Right moves between open apps, Alt+1..9 jumps to the nth open

  the open apps" -> an overview: one key (Hyprland takes Super, so pick one

  the board) opens a grid of open apps as live-ish cards; click, arrows or
  number to pick, Esc to close. [core]
- **A13** "the forge way of showing how many apps isn't really good, it's
  just a circle with a number and I don't remember" -> replace the numbered
  circle with something recognisable per app: the app's accent colour and
  icon, a small animation for "working"/"has news", so he can tell open apps
  apart at a glance. [core]
- **A4** "I would also like a zen mode for forge itself" -> `~/Forge/docs/plans/PLAN-hub-next.md`
  item 4: home screen without apps (wallpaper, effects, clock, focus timer,
  one-line intention, optional ambient sound); a bar button or key enters,
  Esc leaves; tiles, dock and bar hide. [core]
- **A10** "item 5: some apps and sub apps still don't make much sense and
  there are so many that I don't really want them on the home screen; I like
  the background and particles" -> declutter the home screen: fewer tiles
  (for example pinned favourites only, the rest in an "All apps" drawer or
  groups), sensible groups (`~/Forge/docs/plans/PLAN-hub-next.md` item 5 has candidate pairs).
  Keep the wallpaper and particles as they are unless an improvement is
  clearly better; he likes them. **Show Emerson 2-3 layouts as headless

- **A14** "the entire forge app smoother, like pregenerated; I don't want to

- **A3** "the hub, slash work on forge in general" -> after the above, work

## Plan (S2, 2026-09-25)
- A15: App size = WebKit page zoom on the one hub view (forge.py
  `set_zoom_level`), so hub and every app re-lay out instead of stretching;
  CSS `zoom` in shared/forge_chrome.js removed (the cause of the sideways
  scroll). Browser-view rects scaled by the zoom. Text size = new
  `text_scale`: a shared script rewrites px font sizes into
  `calc(px * var(--forge-text))` in hub and apps (fonts are px nearly
  everywhere, so root font-size alone does nothing). Icon size = `icon_scale`
  (hub tiles, bar markers, dock). Presets slider (Small .. Extra large plus
  saved customs) and "Save as custom"; in Settings > Look and an "Aa" bar
  popover.
- A16: Full screen + Close move into the hub bar next to the title (the
  floating in-app pills go; apps with their own header button keep an inline
  Close beside it). Alt+F and F11 toggle fullscreen; plain f removed. Leaving
  fullscreen restores the window's previous Hyprland state.
- A11 Alt+Left/Right, Alt+1..9 in forge.py's key handler, from a key table in
  settings (rebindable). A12 Alt+Space overview with last-seen snapshots.
  A13 icon row replaces the numbered circles. A4 zen: Alt+Z / bar button,
  big clock, effects, sound picker; Esc leaves. A10 layouts to the board
  first. A14 measure, fix, before/after numbers. A3 improve-next list.
