(served by jsDelivr), which repackage these models unchanged.

- chitose, epsilon2_1, haruto, hibiki, hijiki, izumi, koharu, shizuku,

- unitychan: Unity-chan, © Unity Technologies Japan/UCL, under the Unity-Chan

Not taken from the same source: game or anime character rips and packages
