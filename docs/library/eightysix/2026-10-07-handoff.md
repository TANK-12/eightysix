# Eighty Six: handoff, every system rated honestly

date: 2026-10-07 · for: both (Emerson, and any Claude session with no history) · written by Ami (Relay 65)

Where the game stands at the end of the alpha 0.1 wave (2026-09-29 to 2026-10-07, over 40 logged sessions, 169 commits). How to run,
test and build it: `2026-10-07-guide.md`. What to do next: `2026-10-07-roadmap-to-beta.md`.

## How to read the ratings

- **Bare bones:** it exists and runs, but it is a first pass or a stub. You would notice it is thin within minutes.
- **Working:** does its job, tested, but rough edges a player hits, or known holes.
- **Almost polished:** a player would call it finished; what is left is small.

**Verified** means a test or a bot run checked it (named). **Reasoned** means it was read in code or inferred
and nobody ran it. Emerson's own playtests are the real judge. His last notes were on 2026-10-03 (after playing
013: "OK 3rd person main gun aiming, but could still be better; it's fine", then the grapple-line and cockpit-view
notes after 015). Everything since, versions 016-018 and today's look work, is checked by tests and bot screenshots
only, not yet by him.

Words used below (AP, HE, lane, bisect, collider and so on) are explained in the glossary at the end of
`2026-10-07-guide.md`.

## The systems in detail

### Movement and physics: almost polished
The Juggernaut weighs its realistic 5,423 kg. Walk, run any direction, gallop to 70 km/h, an analog slide on
Ctrl / L2, a charged jump, flips (one turn with the stick pushed), air control only from swinging the legs
(angular momentum, 4-6 deg/s) plus the gun's recoil and the wire. Belly clears flat ground at a gallop. Legs no
longer cross or clip through the floor (CLIP: 528 under-ground leg ticks to 0).
- Verified: `test_physics`, `test_feel`, `test_slide`, `test_flip`, `test_clip` (20/20), `test_leg_gap`; bot
  `score_move` writes the movement scoreboard.
- Known: since version 013 the scoreboard's air-turn reads about 80 deg/s (it was 3.5-7.3) and 1-2 belly scrapes
  appear right after the slide, repeatable with the squad off. Cause not found (results/A5.md "Open"; plan: bisect).
- Known: the legs' hitboxes follow the pose, but legs do not collide with the world (only the hull does).

### Standing back up: working, in flight (not committed)
Version 018 stands up by setting lift and roll speeds directly (Relay 58), which breaks the physics-only rule.
Relay 62 rewrote it so the
legs plant and lever the body over with forces only (`standin_mech.gd`, `tuning.json` standin.righting, PLAYTEST
row for U). That work is **uncommitted** in the tree and Relay 62 is stopped.
- Verified by its session: `test_righting` 12/12 twice; bot `righting` stands from 138 degrees of roll (images/622).
- **Open bug:** the full suite at 21:31 on 2026-10-07 had 1 failure, `test_righting.gd:
  test_a_second_try_is_taken_while_it_slides_down_a_steep_slope` (second press not taken while sliding at
  13.1 m/s). It passed alone earlier, so it is likely order- or timing-dependent. Not diagnosed.
- Relay 62's own "not fixed" list: no cap on the hip's torque; the test bench's joint limit is not applied to the
  push; the push starts the tick ground is found, before the drawn foot gets there; the stand-in mech (bench
  option) was seen failing from its back; no test checks that the force acts at the foot.

### Ledge climbing: working
Walk into a ledge the legs can reach and the Juggernaut hauls itself over it (`scripts/mech/mech_climb.gd`,
tuning "climb"). Verified: `test_grapple` ledge climb, `test_mech`. Reasoned: it lifts the chassis toward a
target climb speed, the same kind of help Relay 62 removed from standing up; worth checking against the
physics-only rule.

### Hardcore manual walking: bare bones
Settings > Controls > Hardcore manual walking: each leg lifts on its own button and the stick shifts your weight
(its own bindings page). Exists and is tested for wiring only; nobody has reported playing it.

### Test bench mode: working
Two grounds (flat pad, rough field) that read torque, power, heat and failures per joint. Verified: 29 bench
tests, bot `bench_check`. Reasoned: every number in `data/bench.json` is a draft, and the motors in `parts.json`
are sized for an older 12.25 t mech, so heat rarely matters at the real 5.4 t (B6.md). Re-sizing them waits on
Emerson.

### Combat: working
57 mm gun with real ballistics (drop, flight time, AP dart, heavy AP, HE with real blast push), ricochets off
hard ground at shallow angles, near-miss crack, shell tracers and big HE effects (sliders). Damage is per part
with armour in mm, so where you hit matters (shoot a Grauwolf's rocket pod, the Löwe's knees). Sub-arm 12.7 mm
guns. Resonance blade (hold X at the blade tips, release on green for up to 3x damage). After-action card
grades S to D.
- Verified: `test_combat`, `test_damage`, `test_ricochet`, `test_combat_fx`, `test_resonance_blade`,
  `test_after_action`; bots `combat_check`, `a7_features`.
- Aim: after 012 Emerson said the third-person mark "locks onto the hills"; after 013 he called it "OK, could
  still be better; it's fine", and later parked aiming until after alpha 0.1. Nobody has measured where shells
  land against the crosshair (the planned crosshair-landing bot was never built).
- The cockpit one-shot he hit in 010 is fixed (cockpit parts 1000 -> 2000 health). Balance overall is a first
  pass; "Damage you take" in Settings scales it.
- No recoil reduction of any kind exists, by Emerson's rule.

### Wire anchor (grapple): working, frozen
Fires where you look, real winch (120 kW) and cable (breaks at 170 kN), abseil by paying out; the dart rides on
what it bit. **Emerson: don't change the grapple or the middle-click binding for now.**

### Legion AI: working, rough
Three types with their own brains (`scripts/ai/`): Ameise scouts keep their distance and call others, Grauwolf
packs flank, fire rockets in pairs then charge with blades, the Löwe ranges its 120 mm (first round brackets,
the next lands). Sight cones, line of sight, hearing (a 57 mm shot carries three times a footstep), memory of
where they last saw you. A* path-finding on the big map, bridges as corridors that close when a span falls,
fall damage.
- Verified: `test_ai` (25), `test_legion`, `test_nav`, `test_mission`; bots `a4_mission`, and `b5_finale_long`
  with `tools/check_arrival.py` (the Frontier sweep's last wave reached you, twice in a row).
- **Stale:** after those arrival runs, B5 part 7 changed the units' slope margin and the finale was never run
  again; units may now back away from steep hillsides they used to walk. Dropping the bridge under the crossing
  column with real shells (rather than the scripted hit) was also never run, and nobody has played the bridge
  beat by hand.
- Known: on the small map there is no path grid, so units sometimes pile into one shell crater (1 in 24 flipped
  after the slope fix). Map-edge wall climbing (Emerson, 010) was never reproduced. **Legion accuracy**: they hit
  almost every shot in 012; four fixes were offered and wait on Emerson's pick.

### Legion models: bare bones to working
The three exports from `~/Models/legion` have real colliders, masses, joints and weak points, and LOOK3 gave them
worn liveries (Settings > Gameplay > Legion livery). The shapes are still the first blockout: the Grauwolf's body
is a box where the anime has a swept V head, the Löwe turret is boxy, the Ameise lacks its ringed sensor
(results/B2.md "Next"). Their masses are reasoned estimates, not taken from the anime or a source (B2.md).

### Juggernaut model: almost polished
Export v7 (m39, 2026-10-07): real hip, knee and ankle joints, sole sockets, per-part convex colliders, worn
triplanar metal, emblem decal, dirt that builds up over a sortie. Remaining critic fixes are small and taken
one per session (thigh ram, snout brow and hood pipe, gun-mount arch; results/B1.md). Verified: `test_juggernaut`,
`test_models`, game Juggernaut tests 78/78 at m39.

### Missions and progression: bare bones
Two missions: **First contact** (Ashen valley, three waves) and **Frontier sweep** (4 km map, last wave crosses the
Ash river bridge, drop a span under it). Palouse, Allegheny and Brilon are free roam only. No campaign, no
unlocks, no save game: the only things kept between runs are settings and personal bests (`records.cfg`).
Handler radio calls the waves (text only, no voice). The tutorial is a seven-step stub, off by default.
This is the thinnest part of the game, and the main reason it reads as bare bones.

### Squad and Handler view: working
Spearhead wingmen are full-physics Juggernauts flown by AI through the same controls; orders 1-4 and the
Handler view (Tab: click to move or attack). They only fire when the shell would really land and no friend is
in the line. Two wingmen cost no measurable fps. Verified: `test_squad` 11/11, bot `a5_squad`.
Missing: wingmen do not slide, jump or use the wire; the Handler view has no pan, zoom, contact list or
waypoints; no long-range pathing for them on the big map.

### Maps: working
- **Ashen valley** (hand-made, 1 km): ruined town, river gorge, craters, outcrops. The most finished map.
- **Sector 86 frontier** (4 km, streamed in tiles on worker threads): four towns, river, lake, the bridge.
  Its far crags read blue-grey and spiky; its towns are thinner than Brilon's.
- **Palouse grassland, Allegheny forest, Brilon** (real places from USGS / NRW elevation data and OpenStreetMap):
  rolling wheat hills; about 100,000 firs with light shafts; a 6,000-house Sauerland town. Free roam only.
- Test range and two test bench grounds (bench mode reads torque, power, heat and failures).
- Verified: `test_small_map`, `test_big_map`, `test_real_maps`, `test_big_bridge`; bots per map (images/548-553).
- Known: the ruined tower on Ashen valley is a stack of cubes; trees do not break; Allegheny on High runs
  25.8 fps at spawn and about 31 in the trees. Real-map terrain comes from public elevation data (USGS for the
  US maps, NRW for Brilon) and OpenStreetMap, whose licence (ODbL) needs a credit line in anything shared.

### Destruction: working
On Ashen valley and Sector 86 buildings are made of kit pieces with hit points: the hit piece and its neighbours
break into debris, unsupported floors fall, below 35 % a building collapses. **On Allegheny and Brilon the houses
and props are solid but do not break** (MAPS2: hundreds of them would take seconds to build as breakables). Bridge spans need both steel bearings (marked on the HUD).
Verified: `test_destruction` (10), `test_big_bridge` (9), bots `b5_destroy`, `b5_bridge_drop`.

### The look: working, close to polished in places
Four look passes since Emerson's "too washed out" (2026-10-03): the cause of the wash-out was found and fixed
(tone mapping white point never set), CC0 ground and wall materials with wear, a sky and weather per map,
volumetric fog with light shafts in the forest, worn-metal mechs, scorch and crack decals. Alpha 0.1.1 work
(today, committed 23c2c68, not yet in a saved version): a rebuilt sky (blue, real cumulus with grey bellies), a
relit Hangar showroom, fuller straw-coloured grass about 1.6x as dense.
- Verified: bot before/after sets (images/623-626 before, 629-641 after for sky, grass and Hangar), `test_look`,
  `test_look3`, `test_tone`, `test_world_look`, `test_mech_look`, `test_hangar` all green 2026-10-07.
- Reasoned, not shot: the final small softening of the straw colour (after images/641).
- Weak spots a player sees: big-map crags blue-grey and spiky; Allegheny's into-the-sun ridge view blows out
  near the sun; far shrubs read as dark blobs; grass stops at 60 m (the ground texture carries on, but the edge
  shows on open hills); open Allegheny views greyer than before LOOK3; boxy Legion shapes (above).

### Sound and music: almost polished
Every sound is a real recording picked from 1,816 downloads (mostly CC0; a few, such as the long-echo gun
explosion, are under the Mixkit free licence, and `assets/SOURCES.md` has no sound section yet), played in 3D, arriving at the speed of sound,
muffled behind hills and walls; reverb follows town or valley. The 86 OST plays in order by default (personal use
only, kept out of share zips); your own music folder works too. Rumble on the PS5 pad is safe (every effect has
an end) but is **off in Emerson's saved settings**. Verified: `test_audio`, `test_legion_sounds`, `test_rumble`
18/18, `tools/audio` tests. Missing: voices (the radio is text).

### Menus, settings, Hangar, HUD: almost polished
Main menu with the Juggernaut standing behind it, Settings with a search box (every setting and binding), two
UI themes, controller support in menus, the Hangar (five light presets, orbit, part picking), combat HUD,
pause, loading screen, photo mode, end card. Verified: `test_ui`, `test_settings`, `test_settings_search`,
`test_settings_screen`, `test_hangar`, bot `smoke`. The tutorial is a stub (above).

### Cameras: working
Chase, cockpit (from the front optical sensor, whole mech drawn), free look, optics zoom 1x-10x, Handler view,
final-kill slow-motion cam, photo mode. Camera no longer goes into the floor (CLIP: 170 bad poses to 0).
Not checked: shadow shimmer in cockpit view in motion.

### Frame rate: working
Test range, 1920x1080, Iris Xe, all presets in one sitting (2026-10-07): **Low 66.5 · Medium 58.4 · High 35.2 ·
Ultra 15.1 fps**. Per map before the 0.1.1 look work (spawn chase view): Low 44-70, Medium 35-50, High 22-37,
Ultra 15. Ultra sits on a 15 fps frame-pacing floor on every map (cause not found). Emerson's rule: graphics
win over frame rate; measure and report, never trade looks for frames. **Not measured: the 0.1.1 grass density
(about 1.6x) costs some fps; no after-set was taken (fps runs were paused by Emerson on 2026-10-07).**

### Builds, versions, share zips: working
`tools/play.sh` rebuilds and plays; `builds.py save` keeps numbered versions (001-018) and refuses on a dirty
tree; `builds.py share` makes Windows, Linux and macOS zips with install scripts. Verified for Linux (exports
boot). Reasoned only: the Windows and macOS builds have never been run on a real machine.

### Tests and bot runs: almost polished
63 test files, 523 tests, about 10 minutes headless; 116 bot scripts run off screen on the real GPU.
Known: the off-screen bot occasionally stalls (it detects this and retries once; cause not found).
The libLLVM workaround from crew note 770 is no longer needed (mesa 26.2.4 is installed; bot runs worked today).

2. Air-turn reading about 80 deg/s and 1-2 belly scrapes after the slide since version 013 (cause unknown).
3. Third-person aim mark locks onto hills; shells do not land on the mark (parked until after 0.1).
4. Legion units pile into small-map shell craters and occasionally flip.
5. Ultra holds at 15 fps everywhere (frame pacing).
6. Off-screen bot runs stall now and then.
7. Allegheny ridge view: sun halo blows out the top of the frame.
8. Version 019 cannot be saved until the tree is clean: `builds.py save` counts untracked files too (list below).
9. Frontier sweep finale not re-run since the slope-margin change (may stall); bridge drop with real shells never run.
10. Legs do not collide with the world, only the hull does (CLIP).
11. Licences: a few sounds are Mixkit-licensed, OpenStreetMap data needs an ODbL credit; neither is in
    `assets/SOURCES.md` or the share zips yet.
