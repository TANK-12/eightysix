# Exo Lab: close-fit baseline numbers

date: 2026-10-08 · for: both · from Relay task 108

The `close_fit` test (`data/tests/close_fit.json`, `exolab/benches/close_fit.py`)
on the shipped suits (profile `baseline`). It plays the six shoulder motions at
8 fps with the fitted shells from `static/assets/suits/` on the VRM body. Each
frame it measures how far each shell sits from the posed skin (negative means
inside it) and how far apart the shells are that move against each other. It
warns under 3 mm, fails when a shell goes into the skin, and fails when two
shells come within 0.5 mm of each other. Depths are only searched 10 mm deep
(`reach_mm`), so "-10" means 10 mm or more (since the critic fixes, a shell
whose points in reach are shallower but which goes on further in also reads
"10 mm or more": the edge rule in `Fit._deeper`). Only the right arm moves (the left
side matches it by symmetry). Re-measure after a suit or skin change; this
sheet does not update itself.

## Rest pose (the bind pose, arms out): matches the fit gaps

Verified by `tests/test_close_fit.py::test_rest_pose_matches_the_fit_gaps`:
no shell is inside the skin, and the median gap of each shell's inner face
equals its sidecar `fit_gap_mm`. The least gap is a little under the fit gap
because shell rims curl in. Shells fitted over shoes, hair or the dress
(boots, stirrups, helmet, exo_lite back plate) sit further from the skin than
their fit gap, because the test only looks at skin.

exo_full, least / median / fitted (mm): pauldron 7.65 / 8.0 / 8, chest
7.14 / 8.0 / 8, back 9.56 / 10.0 / 10, abdomen 4.58 / 6.0 / 6, pelvis
4.76 / 8.0 / 8, upper arm shell 5.57 / 6.0 / 6, upper arm cuff 5.67 / 6.0 / 6,
forearm cuff 4.71 / 5.0 / 5, gauntlet 4.41 / 5.0 / 5, hand guard 3.88 / 4.0 / 4,
helmet 7.86 / 8.38 / 12 (over hair).

exo_lite: arm and leg cuffs 3.75 to 3.97 / 4.0 / 4, hip belt 3.12 / 6.0 / 6,
back plate out of reach (over the dress), stirrups 4.96 (over the shoes).

## Through the motions: both suits fail

**exo_full** (the right arm's shells and the torso shells they meet):

- Into the skin, 10 mm or more (the cards name the first frame at that
  depth): back plate (right shoulder, sideways raise 0.98 s), chest (upper
  arm, overhead reach 0.64 s), gauntlet (upper arm at full elbow flex, hand
  behind the back 1.15 s), helmet (upper arm, overhead reach 1.02 s),
  pauldron (chest, reach across 0.51 s), upper arm cuff (chest, sideways
  raise 0.37 s), upper arm shell (forearm at elbow flex). Three of these were
  re-measured against the whole posed skin with a 50 mm search: chest 10.4,
  upper arm cuff 12.6, back plate 11.5 mm in.
- Arm at the side: abdomen -8.3 into the forearm, pelvis -4.1, thigh shell
  -5.7 against the hand, forearm cuff touches the hips.
- Clear: hand guard right 3.0 (closest at the waist), every left and leg
  shell keeps its rest gap.
- Shell to shell: 18 pairs overlap. The worst are the helmet against the
  gauntlet, upper arm cuff and upper arm shell when the arm goes overhead,
  back plate against pauldron (sideways raise 0.49 s) and against the upper
  arm cuff, chest against upper arm shell, cuff and pauldron, and abdomen and
  pelvis against the forearm parts with the arm down. Overlap depth is shown
  only down to the thinner shell's wall (about 1.2 to 2 mm), so treat these as
  "they touch" rather than as a depth. Gauntlet against pelvis comes to 2.95 mm
  (a warning).

**exo_lite**: upper arm cuff -6.5 into its own upper-arm skin at hand behind
the back 0.76 s (that skin is about 18% weighted to the forearm, so VRoid's
elbow weights pull it into the cuff), hip belt -4.6 against the forearm with
the arm down, forearm cuff 2.5 from the hips (a warning), forearm cuff
overlapping the hip belt (-1.5). Everything else holds its rest gap; the back
plate comes to 5.1 mm from the moving shoulder blade.

**Body self-contact** (both suits): the right forearm goes 40 mm or more into
the hips at the arm-down frames (for example overhead reach 0.00 s), and the
upper arm sinks into the head at raised 170°. This is a limit of the
pose, not the suit (the forearm bone itself runs 13 to 18 mm inside the
torso skin there, and the deepest sample is 100% on the forearm bone). Suit contacts within 30 mm of it, or where the arm
skin under the contact is already inside the torso, are not counted.

## Run time

After the critic fixes (session 5, laptop load about 4): exo_full 4.6 s,
exo_lite 1.6 s wall for the whole run (137 frames); pytest's exo_full run
took 4.8 s of CPU. Older notes follow.

exo_full is about 40e9 CPU instructions. That came to 7 s on a performance
core with the laptop under load 8, and 12 to 16 s at load 15 to 17 (other
sessions' headless Chrome). exo_lite takes about a third of that. Session 3's
version took about 112e9 instructions. What made the difference: each
distinct pose is measured once (motions hold their key poses, so 66 of 137
frames are unique), shell pairs are prefiltered together in one array step,
and shells on still bones are only re-checked against the moving arm skin.
The 5 s goal is met only on a quiet core.
`tests/test_close_fit.py` bounds exo_lite at 15 s and exo_full at 20 s of
CPU.

## Critic (session 5)

An opus critic tried to break the test. Fixed, each with a test that fails on
the old code: reach_mm had no bounds (0 passed a failing suit, 100 would run
out of memory; now 5 to 20 in `data/limits.json` and refused in the bench);
profile motion edits were ignored; fps 120 took about a minute (now at most
300 frames a run); a 40 mm push read "9.1 mm"; a stale test code. Also: the
body card blames the pose, still shells read "at rest", rest-built overlaps
show, the page uses the test's warn distance. Not a bug: a cuff pushed 60 mm
reads 5 mm clear because it has left the arm (a ray parity test found none of
its points inside the skin).

## Verified and reasoned

Verified: the rest-pose test above; a cuff pushed 5 mm into the skin fails;
a forearm cuff slid into the upper arm cuff fails as an overlap of the two;
the run JSON carries clash marks with no NaN; signs agreed with a ray parity
test (session 1); bone posing matches the arm model's elbow and wrist within
millimetres; the tester page lists motions, shells and pairs, a pair row jumps
to its frame, and the 3D view puts dots at the clash points and tints the
shells (tests/check_ui.py, 38/38 pass).

Reasoned, not separately checked: which skin is "arm" and which is "torso"
(mean weight over 0.5 on the upper arm and the bones below). At a seam
between the two, a shell point can switch label for a tiny pose change. Seen
once: 0.7 mm on one exo_lite frame.
