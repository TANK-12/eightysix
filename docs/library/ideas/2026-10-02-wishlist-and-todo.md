## Emerson's later thoughts on the physics (same night)

- **Light-chamber laser:** he worried the light loses energy bouncing. Each bounce does lose a little, but
  the best mirrors reflect over 99.99 %, so light can be stored briefly. The real-world version of his idea already
  exists: a **Q-switched laser** stores energy in the laser material, then dumps it in a nanosecond burst far more
  powerful than the steady beam. Real, used in tattoo removal, cutting, rangefinders.
- **Resonance sword:** his refinement: you need samples of every material, density and size to find each one's
  resonant frequency, to smash it or to slice it at the particle level. Real cousins: **ultrasonic cutting blades**
  (vibrate 20-40 kHz; cut food, plastics, carbon fibre cleanly), **ultrasonic scalpels**, and **lithotripsy**
  (shock waves tuned to shatter kidney stones). Limits: real objects are mixed materials, damp the vibration, and
  need sustained driving. As a game weapon: a sword that "scans" a target, then cuts it fast, is a great mechanic.
